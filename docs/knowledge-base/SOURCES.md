# Primary source register

Checked through public web research on 2 October 2026. “Checked” means the page, accessible excerpt, or document was inspected; it does not mean the organisation was contacted. Dates below refer to publication or the period described, not to a guaranteed current service. PDF statistics must retain their sample, denominator, and reference period.
