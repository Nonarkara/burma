# Burmese communities in Thailand: product and service bible

Version 1 · 2 October 2026 · Public desk research, not community fieldwork.

This reference grounds Pirchchat in everyday life in Thailand. It covers work, documents, health, education, money, housing, mobility, language and community participation. “Burmese” is the project's language-facing label; people from Myanmar are not one ethnicity, religion, legal category or literacy group. Do not infer someone's needs from nationality alone.

Source identifiers resolve to the [primary source register](SOURCES.md). Every proposal below is a product inference unless explicitly identified as source evidence. Native-language and community validation remain outstanding.

## 1. Population and geography: design for differences

IOM's May 2025 summary reports an estimated 4.1 million Myanmar nationals in Thailand for **2024**. Registered-worker statistics and estimates of irregular communities use other dates and definitions. They cannot be added together or labelled today's population. National migration reporting describes varied migration motives and service access, with communities across Greater Bangkok, northern, border, industrial and southern provinces. [IOM pathways (S02)](https://dtm.iom.int/dtm-insights/may-2025-edition/data-update-regular-pathways-thailand), [Migration Report (S01)](https://thailand.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1371/files/documents/2024-12/thailand-migration-report-2024.pdf).

Use overlapping task contexts rather than fixed personas:

| Context | Likely product task to investigate | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker entering or changing employment | Understand document route, contract, recruiter and deductions | Assuming one renewal process applies to every worker |
| Long-established worker or family | Health coverage, school access, practical local services | Treating every user as newly arrived |
| Student or aspiring trainee | Language requirements, recognised learning, cost and applications | Equating a short training certificate with a recognised qualification |
| Person seeking protection | Qualified legal help and confidential referral | Treating refugee registration as a visa |
| Resident of a border temporary shelter | Specific programme information | Applying shelter-specific work policy to everyone |
| Entrepreneur, professional or creative | Skills exchange, professional learning and community connections | Designing only around vulnerability |
| Young person travelling without family support | Human child-protection referral | Asking for a public personal story or location |

These are research lenses, not profiles assigned by the application. Offer a manual area selector: Bangkok and surrounding provinces, Chiang Mai, Mae Sot/Tak, Ranong and elsewhere. “Elsewhere” must remain usable. Do not request GPS on arrival. Flow-monitoring samples at selected borders describe surveyed movement; they are not live crossing-status feeds. [IOM flow monitoring (S03)](https://dtm.iom.int/report-product-series/flow-monitoring-myanmar-nationals).

## 2. Work, documents and recruitment

Recruitment costs can create barriers to regular pathways. HRDF's informal-worker case studies illustrate problems such as missing entitlements and insecure conditions, but do not estimate their prevalence. [ILO research (S06)](https://researchrepository.ilo.org/esploro/outputs/book/Recruitment-fees-and-related-costs-what/995218816802676), [ILO ASEAN paper (S07)](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-12/ilo%20-%2016th%20AFML%20background%20paper%20A4_Dec%2024%202024_0.pdf), [HRDF cases (S08)](https://hrdfoundation.org/?lang=en&p=8186).

The useful interface answers: Who does this rule cover? What should I bring? Where can I check it? What costs are confirmed? When does this particular route close? A headline about a cabinet resolution is not enough to answer all five.

The Ministry of Labour's March 2026 extension concerned specified cohorts and deadlines that have already passed by 2 October. Keep it as history, not an active amnesty card. Current rights resources should be linked rather than copying an old national wage number into every province. [MOL extension (S25)](https://www.mol.go.th/en/news/cabinet-approves-extension-for-over-370000-migrant-workers-of-three-nationalities-laos-myanmar-and-vietnam-until-march-31-2026-amid-concerns-over-economic-impact), [ILO worker information (S24)](https://www.ilo.org/publications/know-your-rights-work-thailand).

Proposed work/document card:

- Name the affected cohort in the first line. Show “eligibility not confirmed” if the source does not establish it.
- Separate official charges, optional assistance costs and unknown costs. No invented salary or guaranteed placement.
- Provide a small checklist and official next step, plus a qualified labour/legal contact where appropriate.
- Never ask users to upload passports, permits or employer evidence into public chat or a public exchange listing.
- Preserve the original Thai document name beside Burmese explanation, so users can identify it at a service desk.

Pirchchat should not decide an individual's immigration route. A qualified referral is a product outcome, not a failure of automation.

## 3. Protection, asylum and legal help

UNHCR documents do not establish Thai visa status. UNHCR registration/refugee-status procedures and Thailand's National Screening Mechanism must be explained separately. UNHCR and partner services are free, but registration does not guarantee accommodation or create work-permit eligibility. [UNHCR asylum (S13)](https://help.unhcr.org/thailand/topics/asylum/), [registration (S14)](https://help.unhcr.org/thailand/topics/registration/).

The August 2026 UNHCR update concerns work changes for refugees in temporary shelters. Its scope must stay attached to the claim; it cannot become “all refugees can now work.” The full page needs another check before publishing actionable instructions. [UNHCR update (S15)](https://www.unhcr.org/th/en/one-year-thailand-decision-grant-refugees-right-work-transforming-lives).

Use public organisation referrals, with confirmed appointment rules. UNHCR's directory demonstrates why copied contact details go stale: it explicitly identifies a discontinued AAT telephone number. Never assume an old directory is operational. [Legal assistance (S17)](https://help.unhcr.org/thailand/topics/legal-assistance/), [partner directory (S18)](https://help.unhcr.org/thailand/our-partners/).

For crisis or exploitation language, show a real human contact within the session. Do not require completing a quest, receiving an AI reply, registration, or disclosing exact location first. Model-generated reassurance alone does not meet the project escalation rule.

## 4. Health and everyday care

Coverage depends on the relevant programme and eligibility. UNHCR's healthcare page describes assessed assistance for its registered population; this is not universal free treatment. M-FUND is a voluntary health-fund model serving gaps in migrant coverage. Current contributions, coverage exclusions and enrolment must come from the programme. [UNHCR health (S16)](https://help.unhcr.org/thailand/topics/health/), [Dreamlopments (S21)](https://www.dreamlopments.com/donate).

Mae Tao Clinic describes health, education, training and child-protection programmes in Mae Sot. MAP describes labour rights, community health and own-language information. These are useful public references; neither is an established Pirchchat referral partner solely because its website was researched. [Mae Tao Clinic (S20)](https://maetaoclinic.org/about-us/), [MAP (S09)](https://www.mapfoundationcm.org/en/).

Service cards should expose languages **confirmed**, appointment requirement, public address, documents requested by the provider, cost status, opening information and contact date. Unknown information must be visible before a user travels. Provide list access equal to map access, and a Thai address users can show a driver or receptionist. Never describe inferred city coordinates as the clinic entrance.

Emergency shortcuts can link medical emergency **1669** and social assistance **1300**, sourced to Thai agencies. Do not promise Burmese interpretation, free treatment, or particular immigration outcomes that the source does not establish. [NIEM (S26)](https://www.niems.go.th/1/News/Detail/2677?group=2), [MSDHS (S27)](https://saraburi.m-society.go.th/?page_id=3769).

## 5. Learning, children and youth pathways

UNICEF describes Thailand's policy of free basic schooling irrespective of nationality or document status, alongside implementation gaps. Access policy does not guarantee a place at a chosen school or remove transport and other incidental costs. Migrant learning centres provide additional learning routes, with recognition challenges. [Education knows no border (S10)](https://www.unicef.org/thailand/reports/education-knows-no-border), [Make learning count (S11)](https://www.unicef.org/thailand/reports/make-learning-count).

IOM's 2023 Greater Bangkok household assessment identifies barriers including expense, distance, language and document information. It should guide questions for local research, not be presented as a 2026 national school-attendance rate. UNICEF's border study adds child-protection context for unaccompanied and separated children. [IOM assessment (S05)](https://thailand.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1371/files/iom-dtm-msa-gba-key-findings-thailand_20231107-1.pdf), [UNICEF border study (S12)](https://www.unicef.org/thailand/reports/situation-unaccompanied-and-separated-children-myanmar-border-areas-thailand).

Learning listings need: teaching language, age range, location/remote availability, total known cost, timetable, prerequisites, credential issuer and recognition status. “Accreditation unknown” is preferable to a decorative verified badge.

For the three-dilemma micro-quest, reflect chosen approaches and offer three pathways linked to actual learning resources. Explain tradeoffs without personality scores or clinical labels. Present it as exploration, not an employment prediction. Show results without creating a profile. A saved profile remains a separate opt-in flow governed by the project's minor-consent rules.

Opportunity cards must distinguish individuals from organisations. The ASEAN Foundation October 2026 call inspected here is for CSOs/social enterprises. ASEAN Ahead is a programme announcement; the EYAA cohort inspected closed in January. None supports advertising an unspecified open “ASEAN Youth Camp 2026” scholarship. [Organisation call (S29)](https://itngo.aseanfoundation.org/call-for-applications/), [ASEAN Ahead (S30)](https://aseanfoundation.org/advancing-future-skills-for-asean-youth-workforce-asean-foundation-and-linkedin-launch-asean-ahead-programme/), [closed EYAA call (S31)](https://aseanfoundation.org/call-for-application/calling-all-youth-changemakers-be-part-of-eyaa-cohort-6-now/).

## 6. Money, housing and movement

IOM financial-access research describes documentation, language and other access barriers. A useful comparison should show provider requirements, fees, exchange-rate timestamp and the amount received, rather than promote an unverified remittance intermediary. Bank of Thailand regional payment connectivity does not prove a working Myanmar transfer route. [IOM financial empowerment (S22)](https://thailand.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1371/files/documents/2024-06/pub2023-145-r-fostering-financial-empowerment-thailand-en.pdf), [BOT (S23)](https://www.bot.or.th/en/financial-innovation/digital-finance/digital-payment/cross-border-payment.html).

UNHCR housing guidance describes case-assessed temporary assistance for eligible urban cases; it is not a public inventory of beds. Never place protected accommodation or residents on the map. [Housing (S19)](https://help.unhcr.org/thailand/services/housing/).

Housing affordability, deposits, commute preferences, transport access and shared-device practices require more local evidence. Treat these as research questions. A peer room-seeking board needs contact privacy, reporting and moderation; it must not claim hosts are vetted without a process. No exact home address should be requested by default.

For cross-border corridors, separate geography from operating information. A line on a map does not establish that crossing is lawful, open or safe for a given person. Do not imply a route avoids officials or supply personalised evasion guidance. Use sourced requirements with cohort, date and uncertainty; otherwise route to qualified help.

## 7. Language, belonging and cultural usability

Unicode's Myanmar FAQ describes a script used for many languages and explains Zawgyi incompatibility. Burmese-language UI is necessary for this project, but Burmese fluency cannot be assumed for every Myanmar community. Automatic encoding conversion can damage minority-script text. [Unicode (S28)](https://www.unicode.org/faq/myanmar.html).

Provide Burmese-first controls, optional English, and Thai names/addresses where they help users interact locally. Preserve original news wording and show the status of Burmese translation. Machine translation is not a native review. An optional conversion preview may help with pasted Zawgyi; never silently rewrite a chat message.

Do not equate Myanmar identity with Buddhism or monastic education. Keep monastic learning hubs as one route alongside secular, vocational and community routes. This is an inclusion rule, not a claim about religious demographics.

Community participation should include teaching skills, learning together, creative work and ordinary social connection. The balance of channels, family involvement, audio preferences, and culturally natural Burmese labels are hypotheses to test. Do not invent “community habits” from national stereotypes.

## 8. Trust and information design

Use scoped statuses: **official source**, **provider information**, **reported**, **not confirmed**, **expired**, **corrected**. A rumor may be called debunked only when specific corrective evidence addresses the specific claim. An official source can still be outdated or apply to a narrow cohort.

An actionable brief should contain three source-supported parts: affected people; useful next step; deadline/cost or the explicit unknown. Keep the original article and evidence adjacent. If only a headline was fetched, say so and avoid inventing a detailed summary. Future AI synthesis needs provenance per claim and a safe failure mode; until a reviewed pipeline exists, structured editorial briefs are more honest.

## 9. Community validation and unresolved questions

Recruit adults through willing community organisations; do not approach people based on inferred status. Begin with task walkthroughs using fictional situations, not document collection. Participation should not affect service access. Children require the project's consent and safeguarding arrangements before research or data collection.

Suggested first tasks: find healthcare that may serve you; understand whether a policy applies; find a genuine current learning call; locate labour help without exposing identity; complete a quest and share a skill safely. Invite workers, students, caregivers and different language backgrounds across Bangkok, Chiang Mai and border communities. This is a proposed research sample, not completed fieldwork.

Measure completion, wrong eligibility interpretations, time to identify the next step, Burmese comprehension, accidental disclosure and recovery from poor connectivity. Do not use click-through alone as evidence of safety or usability. Record findings in aggregate without passport status, employer identity, exact housing location or personal crisis accounts.

Outstanding: native wording review; confirmed human referral arrangements; real device rendering; precise public-service geocoding; current policy cohort requirements; connectivity and accessibility fieldwork; moderation capacity. “Maximise usability” requires these checks after the interface changes, not just a visual redesign.
