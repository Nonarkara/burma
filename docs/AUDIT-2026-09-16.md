- Custom hostname does not resolve from this machine; Pages production address does.

## Limits of this repair
The guide and static news still require source-by-source and Burmese-native review. News is explicitly an unverified preview, not a live digest. No account authentication, verified youth/guardian onboarding, private chat, or clinical mentor is implemented. Random browser IDs and nicknames are not authentication. Public messages persist in D1; this is not an aggregate-only telemetry architecture. The project's governance must be satisfied before expanding the public pilot. The unused Next.js scaffold and the separate historical room demo have not become full production surfaces. Physical iOS Safari and low-end Android testing remain outstanding.
